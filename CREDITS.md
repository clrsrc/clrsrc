ideas from. Where we ported or adapted code, the relevant source files are
named explicitly.

this is a direct consequence of the GPL-licensed dependencies and engine code

`tracing`, …) are permissively licensed (MIT / Apache-2.0) and pulled in
transitively, mostly via shakmaty.

## Engines & code we ported or adapted from

### Stockfish — https://github.com/official-stockfish/stockfish (GPL-3.0)
The single biggest influence on clrsrc.
- **Time manager "Mode B"** — ported from `timeman.cpp` into `src/time.rs`
  (log-scaled, TC-adaptive per-move caps).
- **"madd" SCReLU dot-product** — the integer-SIMD evaluation inner loop in
  `src/nnue.rs` follows Stockfish's `madd`-style accumulation.
- **Teacher engine for data rescoring** — `src/rescore.rs` drives external
  Stockfish instances over UCI to relabel self-play positions with Stockfish
  evaluations.

### Stash — https://gitlab.com/mhouppin/stash-bot (GPL-3.0)
- **Time-management formulas** in `src/time.rs`: the move-stability factor
  table, the `2^(-Δscore/100)` score-difference scaling
  (`timeman_scale_score_diff`), and the forced-move detection.

### Viridithas — https://github.com/cosmobobak/viridithas (GPL-3.0)
- **Forced-move / best-move time-management** reference formula in
  `src/time.rs` (Patch B). The continuous variant was evaluated and is
  documented in the source comments.

### Reckless — https://github.com/codedeliveryservice/Reckless
- **Benchmark position set** in `src/main.rs` (the `bench` command's FEN list)
  was adopted from Reckless `src/tools/bench.rs` for representative profiling.

## NNUE training

### bullet — https://github.com/jw1912/bullet (MIT)
- clrsrc's neural network was trained with **bullet**, jw1912's
  GPU-accelerated NNUE trainer.
- The on-disk weight layout and the 32-bytes-per-position training data format
  (`bulletformat::ChessBoard`) are matched exactly in `src/datagen.rs` and
  `src/nnue.rs` so that data generated by clrsrc feeds bullet directly and the
  resulting nets load back into clrsrc.
- The `filter_v2` data-filtering conventions in `src/datagen.rs` follow
  bullet's recommendations.

## Formats & data standards

- **Polyglot opening book format** — originated by Fabien Letouzey (PolyGlot).
  `src/book.rs` implements the standard Polyglot `.bin` reader, the canonical
  781-entry Zobrist random set, and the Polyglot hash.
- **Syzygy tablebases** — created by Ronald de Man. Probed at runtime via
  shakmaty-syzygy; the tablebase files themselves are not redistributed here.
- **UCI protocol** — Rudolf Huber & Stefan Meyer-Kahlen.
